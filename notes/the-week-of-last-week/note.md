It's officially that time of year. The boxes came out, the house got its holiday glow-up, and the star of the show was Juju decorating his very own mini tree. He took ornament placement extremely seriously, including adding his favorite toys and Hot Wheels cars. Jess and I took our jobs as his assistants just as seriously.

It was a small moment, and it's exactly the kind I want to hold onto. It's why I'm trying to document and journal more regularly.

## The little things add up

This year has been a reminder that I'm not just making memories for Jujhar. I'm making them for me, too. And the best ones aren't big at all. They're the micro moments:

- Cookies and milk after a nap.
- A book on the couch while he plays with his toys and asks me to read mine aloud to him. (It's *Project Hail Mary*, and he likes it because there's an astronaut on the cover.)
- A photo Jujhar took of his mom without her knowing: just her, in her natural holiday-decorating element.

Stack enough of them and you've got a life well lived.

Anyway, here's what I'm leaning into:

- **Taking more photos on the Fuji.** Future me will thank present me. I hope...
- **Writing these posts, weekly to start.** Consider this one exhibit A.
- **Reading more:** in front of Jujhar, around him, and to him. We already do this, but as of last week I'm looking for even more moments for us to enjoy learning and reading together.
- **Moving more,** which is extra important for me, starting this Thursday (more on that below).

## 9-5 and 5-9, or something like that

Work has me busy wrapping up end-of-year items; nothing new there. Outside the 9-5, in the 5-9 (as TikTok and Reels would put it: after work, in my free time), I've been learning a ton with Claude. I'm building things, actually shipping them, and slowly wrapping my head around how the architecture fits together. It's been a blast!

## Grand Theft Auto 6, the wholesome edition

Yes, GTA 6 came out, and yes, I've spent a good chunk of time in story mode. Nearly all of Saturday and Sunday this past weekend, in fact.

In a game literally built around crime, I've found it incredibly joyful that it rewards you for not being as violent as you could be. Wrapping a mission without hurting anyone feels like a major win, and your reputation reflects it.

I'm also fully bought into the story, mostly because of the "why" behind the main characters, Jason and Lucia: their kid, Tommy. (A nod to *GTA: Vice City*?) Doing whatever it takes for your family is a purpose that resonates with me.

## What's coming up

- **Run to Feed the Hungry 5K on Thursday.** Will I run the whole thing? No. Will I jog a good chunk of it? That's the plan. Maybe even most of it. I hope...
- **Decorating, round two: the outdoor stuff.** I ordered Tru-Tone lights and I'm stoked to get them up.
- **Catching up.** House chores and end-of-year admin at work have piled up. I need to get back on track here...
- **Next week: Disneyland.** I've never been during the holiday season, so I'm stoked to see it.

## Gratitude, because Thanksgiving

I'm grateful for this season of life, for the time I get with my family, and for all the tiny moments that quietly add up to the life you get to live.
