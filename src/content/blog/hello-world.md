For a while now I've wanted a place on the internet that's actually mine. Not a profile on someone else's platform, but a small corner I designed, built and deployed myself.

So here it is. This site is going to do three jobs:

1. **A notebook for my hobbies.** Things I'm into, things I'm trying, and what I learn along the way.
2. **A log of my projects.** Updates as I build, including the parts that don't go to plan.
3. **A front door for work.** If you're looking for someone to build your website, or you're hiring, this is the best picture of how I think and what I make.

## Why build it from scratch?

I could have used a website builder and been done in an afternoon. But part of the point was to understand the whole pipeline: how a page goes from a text file on my laptop to a server somewhere, and then to your screen in a fraction of a second.

I wrote up exactly how that works in [How this site is built](/blog/how-this-site-is-built/), if you're curious.
